        Scope and limits
        

          This constructs canonical LR(0) item sets and adds reductions on
          FOLLOW sets to form an SLR(1) table. A conflict means the grammar is
          not SLR(1); it does not by itself prove ambiguity. No precedence
          declarations, conflict resolution, lexer, semantic actions, error
          recovery, or LR(1)/LALR construction are provided.
        

        

          At most 24 productions, 12 nonterminals, 20 terminals, eight symbols
          per right-hand side, 80 states, 100 input tokens, and 500 parse
          actions. Empty input is valid. EOF ($) is appended automatically. Item
          paths may contain nonterminals and are not accepted-input or ambiguity
          witnesses.
